# Architecture notes

The standalone loader is `loader/viperport_linux.cpp` on Linux and `loader/` on Windows. `hook/standalone.cpp` and `hook/viperport.cpp` install the engine; the port uses fixed original addresses and 32-bit layouts. `hook/krn_res.cpp` manages resource archives, `hook/wld_cars.cpp` loads the race world and cars, `hook/root_race.cpp` builds the race selection lists, and `hook/menu_view.cpp` implements the viewers. `hook/gl_core.cpp` is the OpenGL renderer. Physics and AI are in `hook/phys_*.cpp` and have replay and shadow checks described in README.

`content_registry` is a read-only, independent C++17 inventory API compiled into the engine builds. It is not yet called by the game. A scan recursively indexes loose car and track bundles, verifies their container structure, reports observed missing track members, and exposes case-insensitive name search. IDs are paths relative to the scanned root; they change when a file is moved. FNV-1a fingerprints identify *possible* duplicates only: collisions are possible. `valid` means structural validation, not a promise that a race will load. Entries and pointers returned by `search` are owned by the registry; do not retain pointers across modification or destruction.

RaceBegin already dynamically grows its car list, but entries and downstream lookups still use fixed-width names. Track enumeration uses `tracks.tab`. The 16 entries in `World::CarList` are participants in one race, not a limit on the content inventory. A namespace-aware resolver, stable content identity, track enumeration and save/network handling must be designed together before changing selection.

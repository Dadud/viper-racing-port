# Mod compatibility

The inventory recognises loose `.car`, `.trk`, and `.tra` files case-insensitively. These are 0TSR containers with a directory and sequential member payloads. It checks bounds and chunk markers, recognises the documented `lockd*.tab` padding used by some community cars, and reports duplicate member names. For tracks it reports the member set observed in the toolkit's samples; this is advisory and does not establish playability. No file is modified or extracted.

Format research: Herb Fargus's [vrmod archive parser](https://github.com/HerbFargus/viper-racing-modding/blob/main/vrmod/archive.py) and [track module](https://github.com/HerbFargus/viper-racing-modding/blob/main/vrmod/track.py) document the container, decoy padding, and observed track members. This implementation is independent; no toolkit code is bundled. The toolkit is MIT licensed. `.mod`, `.tex`, `.cf`, `.stp`, `.ili`, and `.ild` are resources inside containers; this inventory does not parse or validate their contents or resolve their references. ZIP/RAR discovery, installation, missing texture analysis, provenance, and content dependency resolution are not implemented.

The [community gallery](https://github.com/HerbFargus/viper-racing-gallery) generates its catalog and previews with vrmod and preserves attribution and source information. No gallery adapter or downloads are implemented. The [historical tools](https://github.com/HerbFargus/viper-racing-legacy-modding-tools) repository records the provenance of several original and community tools; its contents have not been bundled.
